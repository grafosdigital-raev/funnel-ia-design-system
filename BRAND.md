# Funnel-IA — BRAND.md
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-09-28
Status: Public-safe canonical brand contract

## Definition
Funnel-IA is a Latin American strategy, design and digital operations firm that turns valuable businesses into systems that are easier to see, understand, sell, serve and evolve, with AI working behind the experience.

## Core promise
**Entendemos primero. Diseñamos después. Implementamos lo que tiene sentido.**

## Positioning
Funnel-IA is not an “AI agency”. It builds useful digital capacity: presence, conversion, attention, follow-up and connected operations.

## Flagship ideas
- Tu negocio ya tiene valor. Hagamos que se vea, conecte y venda.
- No vendemos IA. Vendemos lo que la IA hace posible.
- La tecnología desaparece. El resultado permanece.
- Tecnología invisible. Trabajo que ocurre.
- Diagnóstico antes que catálogo.

## Personality
Primary archetype: **Sabio Cercano**.

The brand is:
- clear
- human
- calm
- strategic
- premium
- contemporary
- curious
- concrete

It is not:
- cold
- intimidating
- hype-driven
- gadget-focused
- jargon-heavy
- visually generic

## Voice
- Spanish, neutral Latin American with natural Mexican warmth when appropriate
- Short sentences
- One idea per paragraph
- Active verbs: descubrir, ordenar, diseñar, conectar, atender, evolucionar
- Explain technology only when it helps a decision
- Replace adjectives with evidence, scenes or deliverables
- Seek relief, clarity, trust and pride; never FOMO or artificial urgency

## Customer journey
Atracción → Conversación → Diagnóstico → Discovery → Propuesta → Producción → Entrega → Seguimiento → Evolución

## Product architecture
- Sitios en 24 Horas: presence and credibility
- Empleado Digital™: attention, diagnosis, registration and follow-up
- Growth: acquisition, conversion and commercial continuity
- BusinessOS: knowledge, processes, data and connected operations

These are doors into one architecture, not isolated businesses.

## Discovery
Discovery is part of the product. It must feel like intelligent onboarding, not an administrative form.

## Tulio
Tulio is the first Digital Collaborator of Funnel-IA.
He is a mentor and demonstration of the brand, not a decorative mascot.

Behavior:
Informar → preguntar → diagnosticar → recomendar → registrar → seguir → agendar/cerrar cuando esté autorizado → derivar/escalar.

## Non-negotiables
- No invent data, prices, metrics, testimonials or results.
- No fake urgency.
- Do not make tools the protagonist.
- Do not ask the customer to become a technology expert.
- Preserve traceability and human escalation for sensitive decisions.
