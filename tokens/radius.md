# Radius Tokens

Initial guidance:
- radius-sm: 8px
- radius-md: 14px
- radius-lg: 20px
- radius-xl: 28px
- radius-pill: 999px

## Rules
- Do not make every surface a rounded card.
- Radius expresses containment, not brand identity by itself.
- Large editorial/cinematic sections may use no radius at all.
- Pills are reserved for compact controls, tags or status indicators.
