# Motion Tokens

## Principle
Motion explains transition, priority or progress.

## Initial timing
- motion-fast: 120–180ms
- motion-standard: 220–320ms
- motion-slow: 450–700ms

## Preferred motion
- subtle opacity/translate reveals;
- purposeful state changes;
- progressive disclosure;
- soft ambient-light movement;
- cinematic transitions only in high-emotion/reveal sections.

## Avoid
- constant floating;
- decorative particle fields;
- parallax that harms readability;
- motion that delays access to content;
- animation used only to signal “AI”.

## Accessibility
Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
Core information and actions must remain available without animation.
