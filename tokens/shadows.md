# Shadow Tokens

Funnel-IA should prefer depth through contrast, light and spacing before heavy drop shadows.

Initial guidance:
- shadow-soft: subtle elevation for light UI surfaces
- shadow-medium: stronger separation for overlays/modals
- shadow-glow-cyan: restrained ambient technology highlight on dark surfaces

## Rules
- Avoid stacked “floating card” aesthetics.
- Never use glow as a substitute for hierarchy.
- Dark mode depth may come from layered night/obsidian surfaces and ambient blue/cyan light.
- Validate contrast before adding shadow.
