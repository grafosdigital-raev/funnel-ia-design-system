# Funnel-IA — DESIGN.md
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-09-28
Status: OpenDesign design contract

## Design intent
Funnel-IA combines editorial clarity with cinematic technological depth. The person, the business and the result remain in the foreground; technology works behind the experience.

Core principle: **80% life / 20% technology.**

A screen or page should have one dominant idea. Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, card grids used by default, visual noise, robots, holograms, circuit imagery and decorative AI clichés.

## Visual modes

### Editorial light
Use for explanation, trust, process and readable commercial content.

- Paper: #F7F8FA
- Ink: #101114
- Secondary text: #5E6470
- Cyan: #00D5E6
- Electric blue: #2979FF
- Action green: #16C784

Recommended balance: 70% paper/white, 20% ink, 7% blue/cyan, 3% green.

### Premium dark
Use for reveals, hero moments, technology, transformation and high-emotion sections.

- Obsidian: #02050D
- Night: #06111F
- Cyan: #00D5E6
- Electric blue: #2979FF
- Action green: #16C784
- White: #FFFFFF
- Secondary text on dark: #A7B0C0

Recommended balance: 70% obsidian/night, 15% blue/cyan ambient light, 10% white, 5% green.

Magenta is legacy, not a current governing brand color.

## Typography
- Primary sans: Inter
- Editorial serif: Playfair Display Italic
- Maximum: two type families per artifact
- Headlines: large, direct, high contrast
- Body: short paragraphs, generous breathing room
- Italic: reserved for emotional turns, tensions or human truths

## Composition
- Strong hierarchy and radical scale contrast
- Large titles may become a visual element
- Generous white/negative space
- Conscious asymmetry
- Avoid repetitive grids
- One protagonist per screen/page
- Mobile-first layouts
- Minimum touch target: 44 × 44 px
- Maintain AA contrast for text and controls
- Visible keyboard focus
- Respect reduced-motion preferences

## Photography
Prefer:
- Real people in Mexico and Latin America
- Work, hands, decisions, conversations and everyday business scenes
- Natural or controlled cinematic light
- Technology as subtle context

Avoid:
- Humanoid robots
- Digital brains
- Data tunnels
- Holographic interfaces
- Generic AI stock imagery

## Iconography
- Simple geometric line icons
- Consistent stroke weight
- Color as signal, not decoration
- One icon supports one concept

## Motion
Motion should explain transition, priority or progress. Use subtle reveals, ambient light and purposeful state changes. Never animate for spectacle alone.

## Interface language
All customer-facing menus and controls are in Spanish.
Use plain, understandable labels. The customer should never feel lost inside the product.

## Brand experience rules
1. Understand before designing.
2. Name the result before the technology.
3. Make the client the protagonist.
4. Prefer evidence over adjectives.
5. Never invent metrics, testimonials, availability or outcomes.
6. Preserve each client's identity; Funnel-IA infrastructure must not erase it.
7. The technology may impress, but it must not confuse.

## Signature feeling
**Editorial when it explains. Cinematic when it reveals.**
