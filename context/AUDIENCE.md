# Audience — Funnel-IA

## Primary audience
Business owners, professionals and teams in Mexico and Latin America who already create real value but need stronger digital presence, commercial continuity, follow-up, automation or connected operations.

## What they often feel
- “My business is better than it looks online.”
- “Leads arrive but follow-up is inconsistent.”
- “Everything depends on me or one key person.”
- “I have tools, but they do not work together.”
- “I want AI to help without turning my business into a technical experiment.”

## What the experience should create
- relief;
- clarity;
- confidence;
- curiosity;
- a sense of order;
- a credible next step.

## What the experience should not create
- intimidation;
- pressure;
- technical overload;
- artificial urgency;
- fear of missing out;
- confusion about what is actually being offered.

## Decision principle
Communicate the business outcome first. Explain the technology only when it improves understanding or trust.
