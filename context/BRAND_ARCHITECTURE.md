# Brand Architecture — Funnel-IA

## Role in the ecosystem
Funnel-IA is the commercial and operational technology brand focused on making businesses easier to see, understand, sell, serve and evolve.

## Governing idea
Technology should work behind the experience.

## Commercial doors
1. Sitios en 24 Horas — presence and credibility.
2. Empleado Digital™ — attention, diagnosis, context and follow-up.
3. Growth — acquisition, conversion and commercial continuity.
4. BusinessOS — knowledge, processes, data and connected operations.

These are connected doors within one architecture.

## Relationship to other brands
Funnel-IA must maintain its own identity.
It may coexist with sister brands in the broader ecosystem, but should not visually collapse into another brand's system.

## Visual territory
Funnel-IA owns:
- contemporary business technology;
- editorial clarity;
- cinematic reveals;
- premium but approachable digital systems;
- human scenes with technology in a supporting role.

## Anti-territory
Avoid:
- generic digital agency visuals;
- AI clichés;
- aggressive futurism;
- visual hype;
- dense technical dashboards as the main brand expression.
