# UX Principles — Funnel-IA

1. **Understand before designing.**
   Discovery and context precede solution presentation.

2. **One dominant idea per screen or section.**
   Reduce simultaneous competition for attention.

3. **Outcome before mechanism.**
   Name what improves before explaining how the technology works.

4. **Progressive disclosure.**
   Show enough to decide the next step; reveal depth when useful.

5. **Conversation before bureaucracy.**
   Prefer intelligent onboarding and conversational diagnosis over long administrative forms.

6. **Evidence before adjectives.**
   Use examples, states, deliverables and demonstrations rather than unsupported claims.

7. **Human protagonist.**
   The client, team, customer or real work is foregrounded. Technology supports the scene.

8. **Mobile is a primary experience.**
   Do not treat mobile as compressed desktop.

9. **Accessible by default.**
   AA contrast, visible focus, keyboard usability and 44×44 px touch targets.

10. **Motion must explain.**
    Animation may reveal hierarchy, state or progress; never exist only for spectacle.

11. **No fake certainty.**
    Do not invent metrics, testimonials, availability, guarantees or results.

12. **Every interaction needs a purpose.**
    It should clarify, demonstrate, personalize, reduce friction, build trust or advance conversion.
